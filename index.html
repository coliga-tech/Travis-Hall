Cat. No. IV — Studio

"From the first idea to the finished record."

Every record starts the same way: an idea, alone at the piano. From there it moves through co-writing, vocal production, rhythm section and live ensemble scoring, background vocal arranging, and strings-and-horns orchestration, until a sketch becomes a fully scored, performance-ready record. Development isn't treated as a separate service. It's the same practice, turned toward the artist instead of the song.

I

Original Composition

Full-length songwriting, from first idea to lead sheet, built for live ensemble arrangement.

II

Vocal Production

Lead and background vocal production across soul, R&B and classical-adjacent repertoire.

III

Live Ensemble Orchestration

Strings and horns, scored for a full live band — not a programmed session.

IV

Artist Development

Guidance carried from first idea through a finished, performance-ready record.
